Term Project Topic: Movies

This five-page website explores movies from multiple perspectives including genres, reviews, actors, and industry news.

Home Genres Reviews Actors News

Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to create an informative movie website that helps users discover films, understand major categories of cinema, read opinions, and stay current with movie trends.

Pages Included (Minimum Requirement: 5)

  • Home: Introduces the movie website and explains the project scope.
  • Genres: Describes different movie genres and what viewers can expect from each type.
  • Reviews: Shares short reviews and ratings for selected popular films.
  • Actors: Highlights notable actors and their well-known performances.
  • News: Covers recent movie announcements, releases, and industry updates.
